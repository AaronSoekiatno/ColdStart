About Yondu
Yondu is creating the robotic workforce of the future starting with logistics automation. We're deploying humanoid robots in the first flexible, drop-in picking automation solution designed for 3PLs.
Our approach is building robot foundation models to enable off the shelf robots to do these tasks autonomously. To train these models, we're building our own teleoperation system in house. Finally, there's a whole stack of classical robotics and integration work to make our systems work in the real world .
Learning constantly and keeping up with SOTA research is one of the pillars of our company's culture.
The company was founded by MIT students and has raised seed funding after graduating from the YC W24 batch. Join us in shaping the way the world works.
About the role
Skills:
C++, Python, Docker, Microcontrollers, Sensor integration
We’re looking for an intern to work hands-on with our robots. We deploy these robots autonomously in warehouses so there is a lot on the stack of things to get them working flawless. If you’re interested in autonomous navigation, motion planning, or control systems, please feel free to apply.
What you’ll do:
Contribute directly to our production codebase in C++ and Python.
Contribute fresh ideas and implementations.
Tackle projects that will directly help our team implement a new or improved feature
Build and test software for real robots!

Strong grasp of C++ and Python.
Linux experience, comfortable working in a command-line environment (you know your way around Ubuntu).
You have an interest in how software interacts with physical hardware (sensors, motors, or embedded boards like Raspberry Pi/Jetson).
Experience with Git contributions and Git best practices in a team environment
