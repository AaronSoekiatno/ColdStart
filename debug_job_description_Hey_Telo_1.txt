About Hey Telo
At 👋 Hey Telo, we’re building Voice AI for the Home Service industry.
Every time you flip a switch or turn on the heat, a craftsman made it possible. Germany's 1 million craftsman businesses - and the millions more across Europe - don't just maintain our quality of living, they're building our sustainable future through renewable energy and green infrastructure.

We're building 👋 Hey Telo to set them free.
