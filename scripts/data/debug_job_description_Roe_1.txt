About ROE
At Roe AI, we are at the forefront of revolutionizing data management with our next-generation data warehouse technology. Our mission is to empower enterprises by extracting and leveraging intelligence from both structured and unstructured data at an unparalleled scale. By offering advanced solutions in multi-modal data standardization, classification, inferencing, searching, and aggregation, Roe AI is dedicated to enhancing data intelligence across industries. Schedule a consultation with us and explore how Roe AI can elevate your enterprise's data capabilities to new heights.
We are backed by YCombinator, Gradient Ventures, and Snowflake Executives.
About the role
Skills:
Kubernetes, Python, Amazon Web Services (AWS)
ROE is a hypergrowth startup in the SF Bay Area rebuilding risk & compliance for the financial industry. Today, ROE is trusted by multiple public companies representing $300B+ in combined market cap.
Y Combinator, Google AI Ventures, Ardent Ventures, the CEO of Snowflake, and senior leaders from LinkedIn and top fintechs back us.
What you'll do
Build AI agent platform to serve risk and compliance in trillion dollar financial industry (don't worry if you don't know anything about finance)
Build mission critical features for Fortune 500 customers
Operate as one team: own problems end-to-end, review each other’s code, and move quickly.
What you won’t do
Bureaucracy. No 3-month approvals to launch a feature.
Politics. No fighting other teams for headcount or scope.
Build for the void. We ship what customers actually need.
You may be a great fit if
You’re a new grad hungry to learn, or an industry veteran tired of red tape and ready to bet on upside.
You want to learn how to build a startup from the inside.
You’re comfortable with: Python, Cursor, cloud infrastructure (AWS/GCP/Azure), and agent frameworks.
People describe you as obsessed, curious, and resourceful.
This role isn’t for you if
You’re looking to rest & vest.
You can’t commit to intense weeks (we sometimes run 6-day sprints).
You’re not among the top 5% builders in your network.
You can’t be in the office 3 days/week (hybrid in SF Bay Area).
