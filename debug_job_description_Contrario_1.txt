About the role
About Contrario
Contrario (YC W25) is building an AI-powered recruiting marketplace that connects elite startup recruiters with fast-growing companies through software and operational tooling. We’ve raised $2.5M from Y Combinator and Nexus Venture Partners, with angels from Hudson River Trading, Stanford, and OpenAI. Our customers include 50+ YC and venture-backed startups hiring across engineering and GTM roles.
The Role
We’re hiring
Talent Operators
to run full-cycle recruiting inside Contrario’s marketplace.
This is
not
a traditional recruiting role. As a Talent Operator, you will source, screen, submit, and manage candidates for real startup roles while actively using and stress-testing Contrario’s internal AI and recruiting tooling.
This is an execution-heavy, high-intensity role. Expect long days, high outbound volume, tight feedback loops, and constant iteration. We reward output, judgment, and consistency. Scope and upside increase only through performance.

Responsibilities
Source candidates daily using LinkedIn Recruiter and internal tools (JuiceBox, Contrario AI features)
Run high-volume outbound and follow-ups across multiple roles
Screen candidates quickly and assess fit, quality, and readiness
Submit candidates into live startup roles on the Contrario platform
Manage multiple candidate pipelines simultaneously
Help grow recruiter supply by identifying and sourcing high-quality recruiters to the platform
Actively use Contrario’s AI features and provide structured feedback to the engineering team
Learn what high-quality recruiting actually looks like through reps, feedback, and outcomes
Be accountable for throughput, judgment, and consistency
Technology
We’re building a scalable platform for high-volume data transfers between recruiters and companies. Our full-stack application helps manage candidate interactions, application submissions, and more. Alongside that, we’re developing AI-native sourcing tools, powered by the latest ML research, to make recruiters 10x more efficient.
