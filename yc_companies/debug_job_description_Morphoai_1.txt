About MorphoAI
We’re MorphoAI, a venture-backed deep-tech company working to reinvent the way people engineer robots, heavy machinery, surgical devices, and other complex dynamical cyber-physical systems.  We’re building a new AI-powered invention platform to allow anyone to build embodied intelligence faster, cheaper, and better.
We are a team of passionate engineers, scientists and manufacturers that want to help shorten how long it takes to bring robots and machines to market. We are obsessed with technical problems and much as we are obsessed with customer problems.
Our application areas are currently including robotic system integration, lab automation, industrial manufacturing, and dexterous manipulation.
Our founding team has ex-founders and people from MIT CSAIL, Harvard Business School, JPL, Disney, Intel, VC funds, and manufacturing companies.  We have an office in Boston and are opening up an office in the UK.
