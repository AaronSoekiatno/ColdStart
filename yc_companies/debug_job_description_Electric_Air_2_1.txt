About Electric Air
Electric Air is a tech-enabled HVAC contractor based in Redwood City, California, on a mission to electrify how we heat and cool our homes. We specialize in replacing fossil fuel systems with high-efficiency heat pumps for space heating, cooling, and domestic hot water. By combining expert installation with modern software tools, we streamline every step of the process - from design and permitting to installation and long-term support. Our team of HVAC professionals, engineers, and software developers is building the future of home climate control with smarter, cleaner solutions that deliver comfort, performance, and peace of mind. Backed by Y Combinator, Leap Forward Ventures, and other leading investors, Electric Air is accelerating the shift to all-electric homes.
