Introduction to Superbar
Superbar is the AI-powered search and answer bar built to drive conversion for e-commerce brands. It connects to your product catalog, content, and reviews to deliver fast, high-converting answers that help shoppers buy. It’s platform-agnostic, no-code, and self-improving via reinforcement learning. Backed by top investors, we’ve raised ~$5M to redefine how people shop online.

The Superbar Team
Technology
We're building a search and conversational AI system that needs to be fast and accurate. Our backend is Typescript, using PostgreSQL. The frontend is a lightweight TypeScript/React widget that customers embed on their sites.
The hard problems we're solving include making search work reliably across massive product catalogs (1M+ SKUs) and building AI systems that can understand complex product queries in addition to traditional text-based search methods. Our Answers product helps customers with their questions. The hard problem here is building a system that can learn on its own, what type of answers lead to conversion and continuously improve. This requires building reinforcement learning systems and stitching it with various AI components.
