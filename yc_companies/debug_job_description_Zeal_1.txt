ABOUT THE ROLE
This role is great for those who want to work at a fast-moving, collaborative start-up where new ideas are praised and autonomy and proactiveness are rewarded.
We are seeking a versatile engineer who also has full stack proficiency and enjoys tackling customer-facing product challenges. You will work closely with the Product team to collaborate on requirements and be responsible for the end-to-end design and implementation of the requirements.
SOME THINGS YOU MAY BE WORKING ON
Design and implement enhancements in our web app, which serves multiple types of dashboards tailoring to user types while also prioritizing a clean, simple, and seamless user experience
Build UI components, so customers are able to get started quickly with our product by using them as external modules in their application
Create technical designs from requirements by holding collaborative discussions with engineers and validating recommendations as needed
Ensure our product holds a high level of quality, with adequate tests and debug and solve production issues
Hold technical conversations with strong communication skills while challenging others with a positive growth mindset
Solve for enterprise readiness by solving for scalability, security, and performance by identifying bottlenecks
Design such that the application is functional on mobile devices
