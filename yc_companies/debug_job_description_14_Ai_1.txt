About 14.ai
We are backed by Y Combinator, SV Angel as well as founders at companies like Vercel, Slack, Dropbox and Algolia.
About the role
We are an intense, tightly-knit team based in Lower Haight, San Francisco. Our customers range from fast-growing startups to established enterprise companies like Amplitude and Vercel, and we obsess in listening to each of them and helping them succeed. Our development pillars are security, reliability and performance, combined with pragmatism to always find working solutions and be ultra-responsive to customer feedback and requests. Working both at the infrastructure and product level, we strive to build correct, future-proof solutions with great taste.
As an engineer intern, you will be directly engaging with our customers and will be working with all of our stack, which is currently: TypeScript, Supabase, Next.js, Vercel, and Effect. Topics will include: concurrent systems, modular service infrastructure, text processing, RAG, database optimization, LLM engineering, logging and telemetry, CI/CD, integrations with third-party systems, frontend.
