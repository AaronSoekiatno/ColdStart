About Axross Pte Ltd
At Axross, we specialize in delivering AI-driven HVAC control systems designed to enhance energy efficiency and reduce operational costs for manufacturers. Our solutions are tailored to meet the stringent requirements of industries, where maintaining precise environmental conditions is critical.
With a team of experts in AI, data science, and industrial systems, Axross is committed to providing reliable, scalable solutions that help manufacturers achieve their energy efficiency goals and support sustainable practices.
About the role
About Us:
At Axross, we build advanced AI-powered HVAC solutions to optimize energy efficiency in
industries such as pharmaceuticals, semiconductors, and precision manufacturing. Our
cutting-edge systems help manufacturers better control their production environments while
reducing energy consumption, leading to significant cost reductions and supporting
environmental sustainability.
Role Overview:
As an IoT Engineer, you will play a vital role in deploying and maintaining our AI-driven HVAC
solutions onsite. Your responsibilities will include ensuring that our equipment is properly
installed, well-maintained, and functioning effectively. You will address client concerns, perform
onsite repairs, and coordinate closely with our software and technical teams to ensure seamless
integration of hardware and software.
This role also involves testing and validating our in-house apps and software to ensure optimal
performance. We are looking for a Junior IoT Engineer who is passionate about IoT technology,
enjoys problem-solving, and thrives in a collaborative environment. Your work will contribute
directly to creating sustainable solutions that enhance system performance, improve energy
efficiency, and deliver value to our clients.
Key Responsibilities:
● IoT System Development:
○ Design, develop, and test IoT applications for connected HVAC systems.
○ Write and maintain software in Python, C, or C++ for IoT devices and edge
computing.
● Device Integration:
○ Collaborate with hardware engineers to integrate sensors, actuators, and other
peripherals into IoT systems.
○ Address operating system-level tasks, including driver/kernel development and
hardware-software interfacing.
● Communication Protocols:
○ Implement and optimize IoT communication protocols like MQTT, HTTP, and
CoAP.
○ Ensure secure and efficient data transmission between IoT devices and cloud or
on-premises systems.
● System Monitoring & Troubleshooting
:
○ Debug and resolve hardware and software issues, including network
communication problems and memory leaks.
○ Monitor and maintain IoT systems to ensure high availability and scalability.
●
Collaboration & Documentation:
○ Work closely with software developers, hardware engineers, and AI specialists
to develop integrated solutions.
○ Create and maintain detailed technical documentation for IoT systems, protocols,
and configurations.
Requirements:
● Education:
○ Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, or a related field.
● Technical Skills:
○ Proficiency in programming languages like Python, C, C++.
○ Familiarity with IoT communication protocols (e.g., MQTT, HTTP, BLE) and cloud
platforms (e.g., AWS IoT, Azure IoT).
○ Experience with Linux systems, Docker, and embedded programming.
○ Understanding of networking concepts (TCP/UDP) and debugging tools (e.g.,
GDB, tcpdump, valgrind).
● Problem-Solving:
○ Strong analytical and troubleshooting skills to identify and resolve IoT system
issues.
● Soft Skills:
○ Excellent communication skills and the ability to work effectively in a
collaborative, dynamic environment.
○ Eagerness to learn and stay updated on new IoT technologies and industry
trends.
Preferred Attributes:
● Hands-on experience with industrial hardware and IoT security best practices.
● Familiarity with REST APIs, data management, and cybersecurity in IoT systems.
● Exposure to cloud platform integrations and scalable IoT solution design.
● Opportunities to work on impactful AI-driven IoT projects in a cutting-edge industry.
● Mentorship and career growth in IoT development and integration.
● A collaborative and innovative work environment with exposure to high-profile clients.
● Competitive salary, benefits, and professional development opportunities.
