About Measured
Measured
is a telemedicine platform on a mission to make healthcare more accessible and affordable. We believe in a future where anyone suffering from chronic illness can access a world-class specialist within hours, not days, and via the comfort of their home.
Our first and flagship product is centered around obesity, which impacts over 100 million adults in the United States alone. We take an evidence-based approach to weight loss that is medically supervised and combines:
Prescription weight loss medication delivered by providers trained in obesity, as well as lab tests and monthly check-ins.
A tailored health plan that includes healthy recipes, customizable meal plans, educational courses, weekly goals and a number of tracking tools.
1:1 coaching sessions by Registered Dietitians.
Assistance in getting insurance to cover any medication costs you might incur.
We're well funded and backed by Y Combinator and several other top-tier investors. We've got plenty of runway and are laser focused on building the future of telemedicine, where technology/AI allows specialists to scale their services to thousands of patients instead of dozens.
We're looking for teammates who are interested in being early players and helping shape company culture. We care deeply about a culture that is inclusive and equitable for people of all backgrounds and experiences. If you do not fit this exact description but feel strongly about us and this role, please reach out regardless.

A bit about us:
Measured (
trymeasured.com
) is a modern weight care company. We believe that every person has the right to live a healthier and happier life, and that technology can help us get there. There are many weight care companies that have been around for decades and speak to a different generation. We're positioning ourselves to be the go-to solution for the next 25+ years.
Our solution takes an evidence-based approach to weight loss that is medically supervised and combines:
Personalized behavior change program administered via software. Our proprietary platform provides users with healthy recipes, customizable meal plans, educational courses, weekly goals and a number of tracking tools.
1:1 and group coaching sessions by Registered Dietitians.
FDA-approved medication administered by licensed physicians via telehealth.
We're well funded and backed by Y Combinator and several other top-tier investors. We've got plenty of runway and are laser focused on building the absolute best product to help people effectively lose weight and keep it off.
This is a flexible, part-time contract (1099) role. We pay between $11.50-15.00 per hour depending on experience. We have a target of 16 hours per week spread across 2-4 days. We provide service 7 days per week from 9am-9pm EST, so there is plenty of flexibility with scheduling.
About the Role:
Measured is seeking experienced Patient Navigators to guide our patients through the onboarding process, support them in achieving their health goals, and ensure they have an exceptional experience with our program. The ideal candidate is a skilled problem-solver with a strong background in remote work environments, and a passion for helping others succeed on their health journeys.
What You'll Do:
Respond to patient emails and chats, ensuring all relevant information is accurately documented.
Establish and maintain a feedback loop between customers, the medical team, health coaches, and the care team to enhance the overall patient experience.
Identify customer concerns and resolve them promptly, collaborating with the appropriate teams as needed to ensure a quick resolution.
Gather and analyze customer feedback to recommend updates to product offerings, FAQs, services, and other resources.
Work cross-functionally to ensure that all inquiries and escalations are fully addressed within set timeframes.
Act as an advocate for our members, translating feedback and emerging trends into actionable insights for continuous improvement.
Proactively identify opportunities to increase operational efficiency and boost member satisfaction.
Consistently meet established SLAs and performance targets.
Perform other tasks and duties as assigned to support team and organizational goals.
About You:

You're looking for a flexible work arrangement that allows you to contribute meaningfully from home.

Why you Should Work with Measured:
Huge opportunity. Nearly half of all Americans have obesity and the numbers are only getting worse year after year. Consumers are spending a lot of money on this already for subpar products that don't work well. We have the ability to help a lot of people.
Challenging problems. We're in the business of changing human behavior, which is both fascinating and difficult. If you're into hacking complex systems or your own health, or have an interest in human psychology, you're going to love working on our product.

Culture & diversity. Having worked at high growth, early stage startups in the past, our team understands the value of work-life balance. We take weekends off, go on vacations, and have quarterly team retreats. We’re a diverse team and believe a diversity of perspectives and experiences is what makes a strong team. The stronger our team, the closer we are to our mission.
Resources and growth. We're well capitalized with seed funding and are backed by Y Combinator and other top tier investors. We keep a lean team, and this is a rare opportunity to join us - you'll learn a lot, build cool products, and grow as our company scales!
