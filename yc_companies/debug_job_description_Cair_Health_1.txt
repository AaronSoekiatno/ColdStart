About Cair Health
Cair Health is an innovative early-stage healthcare technology startup on a mission to revolutionize the medical insurance claims process. We are developing a platform that leverages machine learning and LLMs to automate tasks related to submitting and reviewing medical insurance claims. Our platform provides healthcare providers with predictive insights into claims and automates the submission process.
Founded by healthcare and technology experts from UC Berkeley, Cair Health is backed by industry-leading investors like Y Combinator, Foundation Capital, Soma Capital, and CapitalX.
About the role
Skills:
Django, Java, Python, React Native
We’re looking for a full-stack SWE Intern (undergrad/grad student) that will work closely with the founding team to improve and further develop our product. This is a full-time paid internship that will last 12 weeks, with the option to extend into the Fall ($4k to $8k per month). We’re looking for someone excited to work in a start-up environment and willing to take charge to push the product forward.
