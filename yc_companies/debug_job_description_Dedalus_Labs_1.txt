What We’re Looking For
If this sounds like you, we want to talk.
Can you devour documentation?
We’re at the bleeding edge of technology. There will not be tidy tutorials for you to follow. You should be exceptional at reading documentation and raw source code on GitHub.
Can you move fast?
We’re hiring rapid prototypers. You should be inclined to ship rough prototypes fast, and then iterate them to perfection throughout the summer.
Are you comfortable working across the entire tech stack?
We’re a lean team with a lot to be built. As an example, you should be comfortable writing FastAPIs one day, taking ownership of a Next.js frontend feature the next day, all while managing server load balancing, auto scaling, and telemetry.
Do you want to change the world?
We’re looking for a mission-driven engineer who’s passionate about writing high-quality code with an insatiable desire to mold the world through AI. We want to change the way AI agents are used and deployed, with a view of becoming a fully-fledged AI research lab specialized in the mid- and post-training of LLM agents.
