About Planbase
Two decades ago, cloud-native companies rebuilt entire categories of B2B software with architectures incumbents couldn’t match. Those who moved early became the platforms powering the world’s largest organizations.
AI is the same kind of shift today. The next generation of systems won’t just record work — they’ll embed intelligence into operations, adapt to each customer’s workflows, and help teams move faster and more accurately.
That’s what we’re building at Planbase. Traditional software forces customers into a tradeoff: fragmented point solutions or bloated platforms that are hard to customize. Planbase takes a different approach. We handle the hard parts — infrastructure, data modeling, permissions, and compliance — while giving customers the ability to extend the system with custom workflows and apps, generated through LLMs and a simple chat interface. The result is software that can flex to match the unique way each business operates.
Planbase already supports organizations that together touch over 100 million patients. Our customers include leading healthcare companies with $500M+ in revenue and some of the most complex operational needs in the industry. From day one, the code you ship will support these systems and directly impact how providers and patients experience care.

We’re hiring founding engineers to help build the infrastructure behind the next generation of B2B software. This is a high-ownership role: you’ll work directly with the founders to shape the product, the company, and the way healthcare and staffing operations are run.
You’ll be among the first employees at an early-stage startup, which means challenge, uncertainty, and opportunity. This role isn’t for everyone — to be a good fit, you need to be hungry to move build, break and push boundaries.
