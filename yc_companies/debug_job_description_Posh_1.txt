About Posh
About the role
POSH is revolutionizing the battery energy storage industry by providing innovative, intelligent, and sustainable solutions for commercial and industrial (C&I) applications. We help businesses optimize power management and reduce carbon footprints. We are looking for a
Chief of Staff
to work directly with our CEO and leadership team to drive strategic initiatives, streamline operations, and help scale the business. This is a high-impact, high-visibility role ideal for someone who thrives in dynamic environments, loves solving complex problems, and wants to build a category-defining company.
What You’ll Do
You will wear multiple hats and work directly with the founders on business and operation-related execution.
Implement AI-driven initiatives across business functions to ensure we can operate as lean a team as possible. We want to deploy AI agents and utilize as many AI tools as possible to scale the business and operation.
Lead mission-critical initiatives that don’t neatly fit into a single function—from evaluating new markets to launching new products.
What We’re Looking For
Interested in high-growth startups.
Passionate about AI-enabled business.
Exceptional analytical, organizational, and communication skills
Comfortable with ambiguity and can move quickly while staying detail-oriented
Passionate about clean energy, climate tech, and building a mission-driven company
Bonus: experience in BESS, renewables, grid tech, or infrastructure
