Key Roles and Responsibilities
Product Design Leadership
Design end-to-end user experiences for our battery observability platform, from fleet dashboards to predictive analytics interfaces
Create intuitive data visualization solutions that transform complex battery metrics (SOH, RUL, thermal data) into actionable insights
Lead the design of real-time alert systems and monitoring interfaces for fleet operators
Develop executive-level dashboards that translate operational data into business intelligence
User Research & Strategy
Conduct user research with fleet managers, operations teams, and C-suite executives at enterprise clients
Map user journeys across different stakeholder groups (fleet operators, maintenance teams, executives)
Define design strategies that balance technical complexity with usability for non-technical users
Collaborate with clients like JBM Electric, Magenta Mobility, and international fleet operators to understand evolving needs
Cross-functional Collaboration
Partner with engineering teams to ensure technical feasibility of designs while maintaining user experience quality
Work closely with data scientists to visualize AI predictions and battery digital twin outputs effectively
Collaborate with product management to align design decisions with business objectives and ROI metrics
Support customer success teams with design documentation and training materials
Design System & Standards
Establish and maintain a scalable design system for B2B SaaS platform consistency
Create design patterns for complex data tables, charts, and real-time monitoring interfaces
Ensure designs meet accessibility standards and work across diverse operational environments
Document design decisions and maintain component libraries

Technical Skills
