Technology
When tenants, vendors or landlords chat with property managers, they do this via email, phone call, SMS or WhatsApp. Because we’re building an AI property manager that’s a drop-in replacement for existing systems, these are the AI communication channels we’re focusing on. Property management organizations configure our AI using a React frontend app, which is kept in a Typescript monorepo alongside our express server, which are both deployed simultaneously to Vercel, avoiding service layer breakage. We use Supabase as our persistence layer and for auth.
We’re aiming for enterprise grade AI, so we’re using a discrete step pipeline for maintenance issues. This lets us observe and maximumise reliability to satisfy our 99.9% SLA, and minimise the latency between steps, which is a secondary customer KPI of time to resolve maintenance issues (which results in happy tenants).
Our engineering principles:
No ownership silos. All engineers must be able to contribute to all parts of the code base, and must be comfortable with talking to clients.
Fast iteration loops. Quickly delivering feature MVPs to customers has been key to our success so far; sometimes we push small features during customer calls.
Customer-centric and culture of experimentation. If you’re excited about something and want to build it, I’ll find you the time. But let's try out a prototype for customers first.
