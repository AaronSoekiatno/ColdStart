About the Role
As a Firmware Engineer, you will be working with a small team to implement, test, and validate on-board firmware used for Teleo's Supervised Autonomy.
With a high attention to detail, you will be involved in the testing, validation, and deployment of firmware on proprietary Teleo ECUs. Interfacing daily with software, and hardware engineers, you will be directly involved in the launch of new heavy equipment supported by Teleo.
The ideal candidate will have demonstrated experience in developing high-quality firmware on microcontrollers with a clear safety driven passion for robotics .
Minimum Qualifications
Undergraduate or graduate degree in Electrical Engineering or Computer Science in progress
Proven experience bringing up PCBAs, µC and developing algorithms on RTOS
Strong interest in embedded firmware development for safety-critical application
Preferred Qualifications
Experience in board hardware bring-up
History of contributing to projects in C/C++/Rust/Python ideally using software development tools (such as GitHub)
Strong experience working with embedded microcontrollers (Ti, NXP, STM, ESP...)
Experience with protocols/interfaces such as I2C, CAN, PWM, SPI
Experience with Linux (bash, Python, git) command-line scripting and revision control
Interest in developing hardware in the loop tests and automation for manufacturing
Ability to travel to construction job sites
Interest in robotics and autonomous vehicles in particular
