About Interfere
The experience layer for modern product teams – detect, triage, and fix bugs automatically, no human required. Backed by Y Combinator, Vercel, Designer Fund, and many more.
About the role
Interfere is building the self-healing layer of the internet: software that detects issues, diagnoses root causes, and ships fixes before anyone notices something’s broken. The goal is to empower founders, product managers, and designers to understand how the product they're building
actually
works in the real world.
Software runs civilization. When it breaks, the world stutters. We're building its immune system.
This is not a normal job – it demands taste, intensity, and obsession. Difficulty wakes the genius.
The Moment
AI has democratized shipping code, but excellence remains scarce. We're creating the infrastructure that makes product excellence no longer aspirational but inevitable and expected. We've raised over $5.1M from world class investors like Y Combinator, Vercel Ventures, Hummingbird, Designer Fund, and many others to turn our vision into reality.
This isn't about incremental improvements, but about fundamentally changing how reliable software gets built. As our
Head of Community & Developer Relations
, you’ll shape how the world understands, adopts, and builds with Interfere.
The Role
You’ll be the voice, steward, and architect of Interfere’s developer ecosystem.
This role sits at the intersection of product, engineering, design, and storytelling. Your mission is to build deep trust with developers and technical leaders - and turn that trust into a community that shapes the product itself.
You’ll ensure that when developers talk about autonomous software repair, reliability, and AI-native infrastructure,
Interfere is the reference point
.
The Work
You'll operate at the intersection of the impossible and the inevitable. This role demands:
Depth over breadth
– You know the difference between real community and performative engagement, and you build the former
Boundless empathy
– You deeply understand how developers think, build, and debug - and you translate complexity without dilution
Craft at velocity
– High-quality writing, talks, demos, and resources, shipped quickly without sacrificing clarity or depth
"High Standards Win"
– Question everything, rebuild from scratch when needed, never accept "that's how it's done" or "good enough"
Ownership without borders
– Your domain is whatever needs doing to make Interfere exceptional. No excuses
What You'll Build
A developer community that trusts Interfere with production-critical systems
Clear, compelling narratives around self-healing software and AI-driven reliability
Documentation, guides, demos, and examples that developers actually want to use
Feedback loops between users, community, and the core engineering team
Developer education through talks, workshops, writing, and events
Strategic presence in the places that matter: GitHub, Discord/Slack, conferences, and technical media
Early champions who shape the roadmap and advocate for Interfere organically
What We're Looking For
Immense pride
in the work you do and a desire to work towards building the best products on earth
Builders with taste

Systems thinkers:

Relentless learners:

Deep technical credibility
